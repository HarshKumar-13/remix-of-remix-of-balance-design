# Editorial Vision

Create a premium, high-fashion editorial landing page for Balance Design & Fashion Consultancy.

The website should feel like a luxury fashion house / editorial portfolio, not a corporate consulting website. Take strong visual inspiration from these references:

Gucci — https://www.gucci.com/int/en/

Iris van Herpen — https://www.irisvanherpen.com/

Manish Malhotra — https://manishmalhotra.in/

Rahul Mishra — https://rahulmishra.in/

Glamira reference — https://glamira.framer.website/

Overall Design Direction

Create a visually immersive, minimal, sophisticated, fashion-forward experience.

The design should feel:

Luxury

Editorial

Cinematic

High-fashion

Minimal

Artistic

Premium

Confident

Do NOT create a generic corporate website.

Avoid:

SaaS-style layouts

Dashboard UI

Excessive cards

Rounded colourful boxes

Gradients

Glassmorphism

Generic icons

Stock corporate imagery

Excessive buttons

Heavy shadows

Overcrowded layouts

Use large imagery, generous whitespace, refined typography, subtle transitions, horizontal image movement/carousels, and strong visual storytelling.

Suggested colour palette

Warm ivory / off-white background: #F4F1EB

Deep charcoal: #181716

Soft beige / taupe accents

Optional muted brown or bronze accents

Typography

Use a sophisticated fashion editorial typography combination:

Elegant high-fashion serif for major headlines, similar to Bodoni/Didot-inspired typography

Clean modern sans-serif for navigation, labels, descriptions and buttons

Typography should feel refined and spacious. Use large display headlines and small uppercase labels with letter spacing.

PAGE STRUCTURE

1. HEADER

Create a minimal transparent header over the hero section.

Left:
BALANCE DESIGN

Center or right navigation:

Services

About

Contact

Right:
A minimal menu icon.

The header should be elegant and subtle. On scroll, transition into a solid/light background header if needed.

2. HERO SECTION — FULL SCREEN

This is the most important section.

Take strong inspiration from the large, immersive Gucci-style homepage hero.

Create a full viewport-height hero banner using a dramatic editorial fashion image or cinematic fashion visual.

The image should cover the entire screen.

Add a subtle dark overlay only if required for text readability.

Keep the content minimal.

Small label:
BALANCE DESIGN & FASHION CONSULTANCY

Large headline:

DESIGN YOUR IMAGE. DEFINE YOUR IMPACT.

Supporting text:
Fashion, image and design brought together to create powerful expressions for individuals, brands and organisations.

CTA:
EXPLORE OUR SERVICES →

The hero should feel cinematic, bold and luxurious.

Do not place the hero content inside a card or box.

3. SERVICES — INTRODUCTION

After the hero, transition to a clean warm ivory background.

Create a spacious section with all content CENTER ALIGNED.

Small label:
WHAT WE DO

Large headline:

CREATING IMAGE, IDENTITY & IMPACT.

Short description:
Balance Design brings together fashion, design and image consulting through specialised services for individuals, brands and organisations.

Keep this section minimal with significant whitespace.

4. CORPORATE IMAGE CONSULTING

Each service should follow the same storytelling pattern:

Step 1: Centered text introduction

Small uppercase label:
01 / CORPORATE IMAGE CONSULTING

Large centered headline:

HOW YOUR PEOPLE PRESENT YOUR BRAND MATTERS.

Short centered description:
Creating a confident, professional and authentic image that aligns individuals and teams with the identity and values of their organisation.

CTA:
DISCOVER MORE →

Step 2: Full-width image carousel

Immediately below the text, create a large full-width horizontal image carousel.

The carousel should feel editorial and immersive, inspired by luxury fashion websites.

Requirements:

Large landscape images

Almost edge-to-edge/full-width presentation

Smooth horizontal movement

Drag/swipe interaction

Minimal pagination

Small slide counter such as 01 / 05

Previous/Next arrows should be subtle

Images should be the main focus

No image cards with rounded corners

Use placeholder imagery relevant to corporate styling, professional fashion and image consulting.

IMPORTANT SERVICE SECTION LAYOUT

Every service section must follow this exact rhythm:

Large whitespace

Small service number and label

Large CENTERED editorial headline

Short CENTERED description

Minimal text CTA

Large FULL-WIDTH image carousel

Generous spacing before the next service

Do not use a grid of service cards.

The entire homepage should feel like a sequence of fashion editorial chapters.

10. ABOUT BALANCE DESIGN

After all services, create an elegant About section.

Use a split editorial composition rather than a traditional corporate About Us block.

Small label:
ABOUT BALANCE DESIGN

Large headline:

FASHION IS MORE THAN WHAT YOU WEAR. IT IS HOW YOU ARE SEEN.

Description:
Balance Design & Fashion Consultancy works across fashion, image and design to help individuals, brands and organisations create a stronger and more meaningful visual expression.

The consultancy brings together expertise across corporate image consulting, personal image consulting, trend forecasting, line and range presentation, graphic design, uniform design and fashion experiences.

Add one large editorial image alongside or below the content.

Include a subtle CTA:
GET TO KNOW US →

Keep this section emotional, refined and visually strong.

11. FOOTER

Create a large premium footer with a dark charcoal background.

Include:

BALANCE DESIGN
Balance Design & Fashion Consultancy

Services

About

Contact

Website:
www.balancedesign.in

Use large typography.

At the bottom:
© 2026 Balance Design. All Rights Reserved.

Add subtle hover interactions.

INTERACTIONS & MOTION

Use refined and subtle motion throughout:

Smooth scrolling

Image reveal animations

Fade-up text transitions

Gentle parallax on selected large images

Horizontal drag/swipe carousel

Subtle image zoom on hover

Elegant navigation transitions

Animations should feel slow, refined and cinematic.

Do NOT use excessive bouncing, spinning or flashy effects.

RESPONSIVE DESIGN

The website must be fully responsive.

Desktop

Prioritise large immersive imagery, spacious layouts and editorial typography.

Mobile

Maintain the luxury editorial experience:

Full-screen hero

Large readable headlines

Horizontal swipe image carousels

Proper spacing

Simplified navigation

No broken image crops

No tiny text

FINAL DESIGN PRINCIPLE

The website should feel like a visitor is browsing the digital world of a premium fashion and image consultancy.

Think:

Gucci's immersive hero
+
Iris van Herpen's artistic editorial feeling
+
Manish Malhotra and Rahul Mishra's luxury fashion presentation
+
Glamira's service-led storytelling

Do not copy any reference website directly. Use them only as inspiration for visual hierarchy, atmosphere, luxury positioning, imagery, pacing and interaction.

The final result should be highly visual, minimal, editorial, elegant and premium — with the services presented as immersive fashion stories rather than conventional website sections.
